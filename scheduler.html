STEP 03 · CALENDAR

Campaign calendar

Weekly rhythm: Monday WhatsApp Status/content; Tuesday Facebook paid promotion; Wednesday TikTok paid promotion; Thursday answer questions and follow up; Friday Facebook paid promotion; Saturday YouTube video/promotion; Sunday organic recap and review. Paid spending is divided across the month’s Facebook/TikTok/YouTube promotion days; organic tasks are assigned TZS 0 media spend. Important: Facebook, TikTok and YouTube do not have one fixed monthly advertising price. Their minimums, auction costs, audience, duration and reach vary. This planner treats your entered TZS amount as the maximum monthly budget, not a guaranteed platform price or guaranteed result. It saves in this browser and does not auto-publish ads or send reminders while the site is closed.
