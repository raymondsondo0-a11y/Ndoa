Weekly rhythm: Monday WhatsApp Status/content; Tuesday Facebook paid promotion; Wednesday TikTok paid promotion; Thursday answer questions and follow up; Friday Facebook paid promotion; Saturday YouTube video/promotion; Sunday organic recap and review. This planner treats your entered TZS amount as the maximum monthly budget, not a guaranteed platform price or guaranteed result.
