Privacy & Data Protection

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This page explains what the current PRIMONIZER website does with information entered into its tools. Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, identity documents or other highly sensitive information through these pages.

Campaign planner

When you generate a campaign plan, the information you enter is sent to the website's PHP endpoint so it can create the schedule. The current version does not save campaign form submissions in a website database. The generated plan is saved in your own browser storage when available; it is not automatically synchronized to another device.

For public web research, the planner uses only the general business category and an optional city name extracted from your notes. It does not send your business name, personal name, phone number, email address, budget, product/service description or free-text notes to the public search provider.

Contact and appointment forms

The current contact and appointment forms are demonstration forms. They do not submit your details to a server, send an email or confirm an appointment. Do not rely on them to deliver a request. Contact the business through a verified contact channel before sharing personal information.

Technical protection

The campaign endpoint applies request-size limits, basic request throttling and same-site origin checks. Temporary rate-limit files use a one-way hash of the visitor's IP address and are stored in the hosting server's temporary directory. The website also sends browser security headers. These measures reduce common abuse but cannot guarantee that a website is immune to attacks.

Hosting and external services

The website is hosted by a third-party hosting provider. The provider may process technical connection data under its own policies. The site also loads front-end libraries from public content delivery networks and illustrative images from external image hosts. Those services may receive normal browser connection information when resources load.

Your choices

You can clear this website's browser storage through your browser settings to remove locally saved campaign plans. Clearing browser data may permanently remove plans that have not been exported.

Changes

This notice must be updated if the website later adds email delivery, online booking, analytics, customer accounts, payments or a database.
