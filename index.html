HOME / FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Helpful information before you send an enquiry.

What does computer car diagnostics do?

A diagnostic scanner reads data and fault codes from supported vehicle systems. Codes help guide troubleshooting but do not always identify the root cause by themselves.

Can I book an appointment online?

You can submit a preferred date and time using the booking form. The business must confirm the appointment; a request is not a confirmed booking.

Does the campaign planner post directly to social media?

No. The planner organizes campaign ideas and stores them in the current browser. Publishing automatically requires authorized integrations with the relevant social platforms.

Will my campaign data appear on another phone?

Not in this version. Campaign entries are saved locally in the browser and are not synced to a server or account.

How do I get a quote?

Use the Contact page or booking form to describe the service you need. Ask the business to confirm the current price before proceeding.

Are the gallery photos actual NDOA customer jobs?

The current gallery uses illustrative stock photography. It does not claim to show actual staff, workshop premises or completed customer work.
