Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about NDOA car diagnostics, digital ads and booking.

What does computer car diagnostics do?

A scanner reads data and fault codes from supported vehicle systems. Codes help troubleshooting but may not identify the root cause by themselves.

Can I book an appointment online?

You can submit a preferred date. The business must confirm availability; a request is not a confirmed booking.

Does the scheduler post automatically?

No. It organizes plans in your browser. Direct posting requires authorized integrations with social platforms.

Will campaigns appear on another phone?

Not in this version. Campaign data is saved in local browser storage and is not synced.

How do I get a price?

Contact the business with details of the service and ask it to confirm current rates.

Are gallery photos actual NDOA jobs?

No claim is made that stock images show actual NDOA staff, premises or customer work.
